Ownership Chain · LT-0001

The Life of a Legend

1965 — GM Assembly, Janesville, WI
Born on the Line
Rolled off the General Motors assembly line as a full-fat Super Sport, fitted with the legendary 396 cubic inch Big Block V8 and a 4-speed manual gearbox. Royal blue, chrome everywhere, and built for the boulevard.
Unknown — Korea
The Korea Acquisition
Two American soldiers stationed in Korea — worlds away from home — made a deal the way car guys do: a handshake, a fair price, and a shared understanding that this car was worth more than money. The car was shipped back to the States.
Owner · Soldier #1 → transferred to current owner
Post-Service — Charlotte, NC
The Rebuild
Engine and transmission rebuilt from the block up. Upgraded internals, fresh machine work, period-correct cosmetics preserved. Cooper Cobra Radials fitted. Hobart welder in the garage — every inch worked by hand. The Super Sport nameplate earned, not just worn.

How Lemontag Works

01
List Your Project
Submit your car with its full story — what you've put in, what it's been through, and why it matters. We issue it a permanent Lemontag number that travels with the car forever.
02
Get Your Tag
Every car on the platform receives a unique LT-#### identifier. Think of it as a social security number for your project — traceable, permanent, and tied to the car's living history.
03
Sell With Story
Buyers see the full provenance — every owner, every upgrade, every mile of the journey. Cars with rich histories sell faster and for more. Story is the new spec sheet.
04
Keeper's Registry
Can't keep it right now? Register as a Keeper. When your car's next owner decides to sell, you'll get first right of refusal — the chance to bring it home on your terms.
